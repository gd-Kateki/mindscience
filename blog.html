Frequently asked questions

Answers to what people usually ask before their first session.

Therapy is a structured, collaborative process, where a therapist helps you understand your thoughts, emotions, behaviours & patterns, developed effective coping strategies, and work towards meaningful change. The therapist does not make decisions for you or tell you what to do, they provide professional guidance, ask thoughtful questions, offer evidence-informed interventions, and support you for preventative and remedial solutions.

The first session is an opportunity to listen and understand your concerns, goals, and background. It includes discussions about how therapy can best support you along with the flow for future sessions.

The frequency of sessions depends on your needs, goals, and progress. This will be discussed collaboratively with your therapist.

Sessions typically last around 45-60 minutes, depending on the type of service.
