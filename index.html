Supporting your well-being

Mind Science, where your well-being matters.

Mind Science Clinic is for everyone who wants to be Healthy, Happy and Competent. Our well-being is governed by the health of our Mind and hence, it is of prime importance to care for it. The undesirable adverse situations, events, people, and relationships in our personal & professional life and our interpretation of the same scar our mind, creating discomfort and further deteriorate its health if dwelled upon. When not attended to in time, it can lead to mental disorders, disrupting all facets of our well-being viz. physical health, physiological health, metabolic health, emotional health, familial health, social health, financial health, relationships, our overall competence. Being conscious about building and maintaining our mental health can not only prevent or cure discomfort and disorders but also can empower us to lead a life enriched with competence, health, and happiness for ourselves and our beloved.

— Dr. Sunil Dharane ©

Counselling Psychologist

I'm a Psychologist who believes in creating a safe and comfortable space where individuals can openly explore their thoughts, emotions, and experiences. My approach draws from CBT and REBT, along with practical strategies such as grounding techniques, to help individuals manage emotional distress and develop healthier coping skills. I also conduct psychological assessments to better understand cognitive, emotional, behavioural, and personality-related concerns.
